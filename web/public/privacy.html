Privacy Policy

Last updated November 12, 2026

EdgeCall is a free, static web tool. It has no accounts, no sign-up and no contact forms, and it does not ask you for any personal information.

What we collect

Nothing. The game situations, pitch locations and umpire selections you enter are processed entirely in your browser and are not sent to us or stored anywhere. EdgeCall does not use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers or browser storage.

Third parties

Loading the site involves two outside services, each with its own privacy policy:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address when your browser requests a page. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Google Fonts serves the site's typefaces, so your browser contacts Google's servers to fetch them. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Links to GitHub, LinkedIn and other external sites are governed by those sites' own policies.

Children

EdgeCall does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
